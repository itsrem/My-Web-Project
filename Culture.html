Traditional Life

The culture of Al Ahsa is connected to its history, people, and natural environment.
Traditions have been passed from one generation to another and remain part of the area's identity.

Elements of Local Culture

  • Traditional Markets: Markets have long been places for trade and social interaction.
  • Handicrafts: Traditional crafts reflect skills and knowledge passed between generations.
  • Local Food: Food is an important part of hospitality and family gatherings.
  • Date Palms: Palm trees are closely connected to the environment and heritage of Al Ahsa.

Keeping Heritage Alive

Learning about traditions and historic places helps preserve the stories of Al Ahsa for future generations.
