Al Qarah MountainSpecific specific specifications For generations, Al Qarah Mountain has stood among the palm groves of Al Ahsa. Its natural caves, shaped over time by nature, offer visitors a chance to explore the landscape that makes this oasis unique.
Ibrahim Palace Located in the historic heart of Al Hofuf, Ibrahim Palace reflects an important part of Al Ahsa's past. Its architecture combines Islamic features with military design, showing how the palace served both as a place of authority and a military site.
Jawatha Mosque Jawatha Mosque is connected to the early history of Islam in Al Ahsa. It is associated with the Bani Abd Al-Qays tribe and is remembered as one of the earliest mosques where Friday prayers were held outside Madinah.
Al Qaisariya Souq Al Qaisariya Souq is one of the most important historic landmarks in Al Ahsa. For generations, it has been a center of trade and daily life, where merchants and visitors gathered to buy and sell traditional goods. Its covered pathways and traditional shops preserve the atmosphere of the old market.
